================================================================
MASTER PROMPT — Liora Voss multi-view reference set
================================================================

**Use the ANCHOR BLOCK at the bottom of
05_Locked_Visual_Description.md as the opening of every new
prompt.** It is the as-built description pulled from the 5 locked
reference images, not the original spec. It will reproduce the
locked look more reliably than the older block below.

Goal: produce 8–9 reference images that all look like the EXACT
same character from different angles and expressions. Keep the
anchor block identical across every generation so the style locks
in. Only change the "View / Pose / Expression" line.

----------------------------------------------------------------
LOCKED CHARACTER BLOCK (copy-paste verbatim, every generation)
----------------------------------------------------------------

High-detail character reference in photoreal-anime hybrid style.

Character: Liora Voss, confident adult woman, charismatic explorer archetype.

Appearance (must match exactly):
- Messy high bun with soft bangs and loose face-framing strands
- Rich dark brown hair (#3A2F2F) with subtle warm highlights
- Large expressive amber-brown eyes (#7A4B2A), long lashes, subtle eyeliner, eyes ~1.5x realistic scale
- Small beauty mark directly under the left eye
- Full lips, natural slight upturned smile
- Warm peach skin (#F5D0C5) with realistic micro-detail, soft sheen, soft subsurface highlights
- Stylized 1:6.5 head-to-body ratio, hourglass silhouette, long toned legs
- Wardrobe: sheer white fitted tank top + light gray garment with warm gold (#C9A227) lace trim

Style rules:
- Photoreal-anime hybrid: realistic skin and lighting, anime-scale eyes and facial proportions
- Soft cinematic gradients on skin, subtle cel-shading on hair and fabric
- No hard cartoon outlines on skin, no plastic/doll material, no flat-shaded skin
- Background: pure neutral light gray (#E8E8E8)
- Lighting: soft even studio lighting, gentle key from camera-left, no harsh shadows
- Resolution: 4K, sharp focus, full body in frame

----------------------------------------------------------------
CONSISTENCY TIPS
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Keep the LOCKED CHARACTER BLOCK byte-identical across all 9 prompts.
- Reuse the same seed if Grok Imagine offers it; otherwise log the
  generation number for each view in 04_Notes.txt.
- Generate one view at a time. After view 1 is approved, use the
  "edit / vary" feature on view 1 as the base for views 2+.
- If a view drifts (wrong hair, missing beauty mark, off proportions),
  regenerate it — do not move forward with inconsistent references.
- The eye color, beauty mark, and bun shape are the three most
  common drift points. Check those first on every output.
