## 1. Rendering Style

- **Medium:** Digital painterly illustration with **visible brush strokes** across hair, skin, fabric, and background.
- **Brush quality:** Soft, slightly impressionistic. Edges feather rather than cleanly delineate. Hair strands are painted in chunky brush clusters with directional streaks.
- **Skin rendering:** Smooth painterly gradients. **No** real-skin pore detail. **No** plastic CG specular. **No** photo-grain. **No** flat cel-shading either — there is always some gradient falloff.
- **Fabric rendering:** Ribbed cotton suggested through subtle vertical brush lines, not hyperreal weave.
- **Background:** Also painted, with visible brush texture — not a flat color fill.
- **Color blending:** Soft. No hard-edged anime shading. No bold cartoon outlines.
- **Overall reference style:** Premium illustrated character art — closer to polished concept art for a stylized AAA game or animated feature than anime fanart.

## 2. Lighting

- **Key light:** Strong directional, from **upper-left** (or upper-right, mirrored for the ¾-right view), creating a clear lit side and shadow side on the face and body.
- **Rim light:** Warm orange-amber rim (`#FFB773` to `#FFC892`) wraps along:
  - Top of the bun and crown of the head
  - Upper rim of the bangs
  - Tops of the shoulders
  - Upper edge of the bust
  - One side of the jaw
- **Lighting vibe:** "Golden hour cinematic" — warm, flattering, soft. Reads almost backlit, with a thin warm halo on hair edges.
- **Shadows:** Soft, mid-tone, never deep black. Cool-neutral falloff.
- **No** harsh studio shadows, **no** hard cast shadows on the background, **no** rim light from behind the camera.

## 3. Hair

- **Base color:** Rich very-dark-brown approaching near-black (`#2A2222` to `#3A2F2F`).
- **Highlight color:** Warm orange-amber catch-light from the rim light, around `#B86A2E` to `#D88040`. Highlights only appear where the rim light hits — top and one side.
- **Style — bun:** Tall messy high bun set on the **top-back** of the head. Generous volume, not a tight ballet bun. Loose, slightly chunky strands escape outward and upward from the bun.
- **Bangs:** Full bangs across the forehead, slightly parted in the middle, falling to just above the eyebrows or lightly grazing the eyelid. **Wispy and feathered**, never blunt-cut.
- **Face-framing strands:** 2–3 loose wisps escape on each side of the face, hanging down near the jaw or past the cheekbone, with a slight natural curl.
- **Movement:** Hair has a dynamic, slightly breeze-blown quality — flyaway strands lift from the bun, not perfectly groomed.

## 4. Face

- **Age read:** Adult woman, late 20s. Mature but soft features. Never childlike, never ambiguous.
- **Face shape:** Oval, slightly heart-shaped, with a defined-but-soft jawline. Chin is gently pointed, not square.
- **Cheekbones:** Moderately defined, soft shadow underneath.
- **Nose:** Small-to-medium, narrow bridge, slightly upturned tip. Painterly nostril shadow, not detailed.
- **Eyes:**
  - Shape: Almond, with **slightly downturned outer corners** — this is what gives the confident/knowing read.
  - Size: Realistic eye-to-face ratio. **Not** exaggerated anime scale.
  - Iris color: Amber-brown / honey-amber, `#C8741F` to `#E08A1F`, with a darker outer ring.
  - Pupil: Standard round, dark.
  - Eyeliner: Subtle dark liner along the upper lash line with a slight winged tip. Lower lash line clean or barely lined.
  - Lashes: Long, defined, slightly curled — painted, not photographic.
- **Brows:** Dark brown, naturally arched, medium thickness. No harsh angles, no thin over-plucked look.
- **Lips:**
  - Shape: Full but not overly plump. Natural adult proportions.
  - Color: Warm pink-coral, `#C66857` to `#D87864`.
  - Expression: Usually a **closed-mouth confident half-smile** or a soft slight smile. Sometimes lips parted slightly. Never an open grin in the locked set.
- **Beauty mark:** A single small dark dot **directly under the LEFT eye** (her left side, the viewer's right side of her face). Positioned roughly 1 cm below the lower lash line, slightly toward the cheek. Visible in the front and side-left views; hidden behind hair/angle in the ¾ right and back views.
- **Blush:** Very faint warm cheek blush in some images. Subtle, not strong.
- **Expression vibe:** Confident, knowing, mildly amused. Mature poise. Never coy, never bashful, never childlike.

## 5. Skin

- **Base tone:** Warm peach `#F5D0C5`, slightly sun-kissed. Not pale, not dark.
- **Lit areas:** Shift warmer toward `#FAD9C3` / soft golden peach.
- **Shadow areas:** Cooler peach-mauve, `#D6A599` range. Never gray, never green.
- **Surface quality:** Painterly with a soft sheen. **No** visible pores, **no** photo-grain. The "glow" comes from the warm rim light, not from skin material.
- **Subtle freckles:** Very faint, sometimes present across the nose bridge — not a defining feature.

## 6. Body / Proportions

- **Build:** Athletic adult woman. **Hourglass-with-restraint** — full bust but **not** exaggerated. The locked references show a natural, balanced bust silhouette across front, ¾, and side views.
- **Waist:** Defined but not unnaturally tiny.
- **Shoulders:** Moderate width, slightly narrower than hip width (mild pear-leaning hourglass).
- **Arms:** Toned, slim, not muscular. Soft painterly definition on the deltoid and bicep.
- **Posture:** Confident and relaxed. Shoulders square. Never hunched.
- **Head-to-body ratio (estimated from partial-body references):** ~1:7, slightly stylized but not anime-extreme. To be confirmed when the full-body shot is generated.

## 7. Wardrobe

**Top — white ribbed cotton tank:**
- Color: Clean off-white, `#F2F0EC`.
- Fabric: Visible vertical ribbing suggested by subtle painterly brush lines. **Fully opaque.** Slight worn-in soft cotton feel.
- Neckline: Wide U-shape / scoop neck, sitting on the collarbone.
- Straps: Medium width (~1.5 cm), set wide on the shoulder.
- Hem: Hits at the waist or just below the navel — slightly cropped.
- **No** graphics, **no** lace, **no** transparency, **no** sheer panels.

**Bottom — boyshorts:**
- Color: Medium-light cool gray, `#9AA0A8` to `#A6ACB3`.
- Fabric: Solid cotton/jersey, **opaque**.
- Cut: Boyshort / hipster cut, sitting low on the hips.
- Trim: Warm gold metallic stitching (`#C9A227` / `#D4AC2F`) along the top waistband — a thin contrast stripe, sometimes with a small gold side-seam line. **Not** bulky lace, **not** decorative bows.

**Accessories:** None. No earrings, necklace, bracelets, rings, or other jewelry in the locked set.

## 8. Background

- **Color:** Painterly neutral warm-gray, `#D6D2CD` to `#BFBAB3`.
- **Gradient:** Slight, sometimes with a soft diagonal lighter or darker band — feels like a wash of light across a studio backdrop.
- **Texture:** Visible painterly brush marks. **Not** a flat color fill.
- **Environment:** None. No objects, no horizon, no window, no furniture.

## 9. Framing Conventions

- **Camera height:** Approximately chest / eye level.
- **Camera angle:** Slight low angle (1–5 degrees looking up) — gives a confident "presence" feel.
- **Crop:** All current locked images crop somewhere between mid-torso and upper-thigh. **No full-body shot yet.**
- **Headroom:** Modest, not generous.

## 10. Overall Vibe

Premium illustrated character art with a confident, mature, charismatic adult woman as the subject. Warm cinematic lighting, painterly brushwork, balanced and athletic proportions, clean opaque athleticwear. Reference points: polished modern concept art / animated-feature character design. **Not** anime fanart, **not** photoreal, **not** CG render.
